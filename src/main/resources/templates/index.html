Lost your job? You may be able to get weekly benefits.

Unemployment insurance pays part of your lost wages while you look for a new job. Filing takes about 10 minutes, and you'll see an estimate of your weekly benefit right away.

Start your claim Check an existing claim

How it works

  1. 1. File your claim

    Tell us about your last job and why it ended. You'll get a confirmation number.

  2. 2. We review it

    A caseworker checks your details. You can upload proof, like a layoff letter, to help.

  3. 3. Check in each week

    Once approved, answer a few questions every week about your job search.

  4. 4. Get paid

    Each week you qualify for is paid, for up to 26 weeks.
